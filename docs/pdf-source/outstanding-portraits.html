Photo standard
  • Head and shoulders, subject facing the camera, head roughly filling the upper third.
  • A plain wall behind the subject is ideal — any single flat colour.
  • One person in the frame. A colleague standing behind gets cut out with them.
  • Portrait orientation, as large a file as you have. Straight from the phone is fine.
What cannot be used

Full-length photographs, group shots, and screenshots or exports of a designed poster. These have to be cropped down to a head, which leaves too few pixels for the card and often cannot be separated from the background at all. Two chapters have already had to be redone for this reason, and a handful of portraits still cannot be brought fully into line.

Names and offices below are exactly as each chapter supplied them. If any is wrong, please correct it in your reply — including whether a person is styled QS, and their post-nominals.

Where to send

Send packs to the NIQS Portal & Web Page Implementation team. Please name each file with the officer's name and office — for example QS Jane Doe, MNIQS - General Secretary.jpg. That one habit removes most of the guesswork at our end, and it is how the chapters whose pages went up fastest sent theirs.
